What it does

  • Log food by barcode, by nutrition label photo, by voice, or by searching a food database.
  • Build and run workouts, including supersets, with rest timers and personal-record tracking.
  • Watch your weight trend instead of the daily noise, and get a calorie-burn estimate that adapts to your own results.
  • Track measurements and progress photos, kept on your phone behind Face ID.
  • Share your day with friends, only if you choose to.
